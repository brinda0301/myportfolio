F1 2026 Race Winner Prediction System

A dual-model engine that predicts each Grand Prix winner. A Monte Carlo simulator runs 100,000 races across 18 weighted features, and an XGBoost model retrains as new results arrive. Every prediction is committed to GitHub before lights out and never regenerated, so the track record cannot be edited after the fact.

VALIDATION
Leave-one-race-out backtest, 2024 and 2025

DIAGNOSTIC NOTE

The backtest over 48 races showed the model ties an "always pick pole" baseline, because most features derive from qualifying pace. I publish that baseline on the dashboard beside the model, and I documented the root cause and the fixes it led to in the repo.
